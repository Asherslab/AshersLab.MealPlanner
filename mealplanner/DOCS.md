# Meal Planner

Plan the household's meals on a calendar, build the shopping list per store, and see what it costs.

## Using it

Open **Meals** in the Home Assistant sidebar. The app works on a phone through the Home Assistant companion
app as well as in a desktop browser. Anyone who can log in to Home Assistant can use it; the app knows who
you are from your Home Assistant login.

## Your data

Everything is stored in this add-on's own data folder, which is included in Home Assistant backups. Each
phone or browser also keeps its own copy so the app keeps working with poor reception, and catches up when
it is back online.
